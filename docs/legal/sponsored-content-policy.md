## Sponsored content policy

*Last updated: [date of publication]*

TechDirectory publishes a limited number of sponsored posts on technology and business-technology topics. This policy explains what you get, the rules every post must follow, and how refunds work. By placing an order you agree to it.

Sponsored posts are sold to businesses only. You must give your company name when you order.

### What a post must be

- **Sponsored articles:** 100% unique, meaning not published anywhere else, including your own site, and not published elsewhere afterwards. At least 500 words; 1,000 or more is recommended.
- **Press releases:** your company's own news. The release may already be published elsewhere, including on newswires and your own site. You add a summary of 2–3 sentences (at least 40 words) written for TechDirectory, which appears at the top as "In short". At least 300 words. You may not submit someone else's news.
- Either way, the same text can't be published on TechDirectory twice.
- About a technology or business-technology topic that is relevant to people who buy technology for their organisations.
- Accurate. No claims you cannot support, no claims about other named companies, and no fake reviews or testimonials.
- Mainly useful to the reader, not written mainly to carry links.

### What we do not accept

Gambling and betting; adult content; crypto-assets, tokens and crypto trading; investments, trading signals, loans, credit or insurance; CBD, cannabis or other drugs; weapons; hacking or account-cracking services; essay-writing services; fake reviews or followers; health or medical claims; and anything unlawful in the UK, the EU or the US. We may also decline content that we reasonably believe could mislead readers or harm TechDirectory's reputation.

### How your post is reviewed

Every post goes through automatic checks before it is published: length, number and safety of links, link wording, banned topics, relevance, language and format, clean formatting, and your order history with us.

- **All checks pass:** your post is published automatically, usually within minutes, and we email you the live link.
- **A check fails clearly** (for example a banned topic, more than 2 links or an unsafe link): we do not publish it, we refund you in full automatically, and we tell you why. You can resubmit a corrected post once, free of charge, within 30 days.
- **A check is borderline:** a person reviews your post within 3 working days and either publishes it or declines it with a full refund.

Publication is never guaranteed until your post is live. Paying does not oblige us to publish content that breaks this policy.

### After publication

- You can ask for one correction (a typo, a changed product name or a broken link to your own site) within 30 days, free of charge. Send it by email to info@techdirectory.io. We do not make other changes.
- If one of your links later points to content that breaks this policy, or stops working, we may remove that link or unpublish the post.
- We may remove a post at any time if we learn it breaks this policy or the law.

### Refunds

Refunds go back to the card you paid with, normally within 5 to 10 working days depending on your bank. If you dispute a payment with your bank instead of contacting us, we may pause your other orders while the dispute is open.

### Search engines and results

Sponsored links carry `rel="sponsored"`, so they do not pass search ranking. We make no promise about search rankings, traffic or enquiries.

### Questions and complaints

Write to info@techdirectory.io. We reply within 1 working day. We do not take orders or handle questions by phone.

### Law

This policy is governed by the laws of England and Wales. Nothing in it limits any rights you have that cannot be limited by law.
