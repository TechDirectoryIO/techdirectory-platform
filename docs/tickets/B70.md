# B70 · Plan payments on Stripe's payment page (owner approved, 30 October 2026)

**Why.** On 30 October the owner's test payments on the TD platform's own card form were declined (the card issuer, Tide, returned code 51 "insufficient funds" on a prepaid card after 3D Secure passed). The form then made him start again: the "not a robot" tick expired and every field had to be filled in once more. The form takes cards only, in US dollars only. Stripe shows **205 failed payments** in the account's history, most of them renewals declined for insufficient funds, "transaction not allowed" or "do not honour".

**Decision (owner, "yes"):**
1. Switch on more payment methods in Stripe.
2. Move membership plan payments from the TD platform's card form to Stripe Checkout and Stripe Billing.

This changes Build Scope 17 (billing stayed inside the TD platform).

## What members get

| Problem today | With B70 |
| --- | --- |
| Cards only | Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link (one-click saved card), Revolut Pay, PayPal, Bacs Direct Debit (UK) and SEPA Direct Debit (EU) for plans, as switched on in Stripe |

## How it works

`automations/cloudflare/td-billing` (Cloudflare Worker, free plan). Tests: `node test/worker.test.mjs`, **22 of 22**. The Wrangler dry run bundles to 10 KB.

- **"Upgrade" button** (`site/templates/b70-upgrade-buttons.html`): sends the signed-in member's ID, the plan, monthly or yearly, and the visitor's currency. The Worker reads the member's email from the TD platform (never from the browser), finds or creates their Stripe customer, and opens Stripe's payment page.
- **Paid by card or wallet:** the member is moved to the plan straight away through the TD API.
- **Paid by Direct Debit:** the plan changes when the payment clears (a few days).
- **Plan changed in the Stripe portal:** the TD plan follows the price.
- **Renewal fails:** Stripe retries; the member keeps the plan meanwhile. When the retries run out, or the member cancels, they go back to Basic.
- **Safety:**
  - Stripe signatures are checked and replays refused.
  - Each event is acted on once.
  - The plan changes only for the member who owns the Stripe customer, so a hand-edited member ID changes nothing.
  - If the TD platform is down, Stripe retries for up to 3 days.

## Existing paying members

They stay on the TD platform's billing until they choose to move. Their next upgrade or "Manage billing" goes through Stripe. Nobody is charged twice: an old platform subscription is cancelled only after the member's new Stripe subscription is active.

## Go-live (change window, after B46)

1. **Stripe → Settings → Payment methods** (owner approved). Switch on Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link, PayPal, Bacs Direct Debit and SEPA Direct Debit for Checkout. Apple Pay and Google Pay need no domain check on Stripe's own payment page.
2. **Stripe → Settings → Billing:**
   - Smart Retries on (8 attempts over 2 weeks), then cancel the subscription.
   - Emails for failed payments and expiring cards on.
   - Customer portal on (update card, invoices, cancel, switch between Featured and Premium); copy its login link into `PORTAL_LOGIN_URL`.
3. **Stripe → Product catalogue:** products and prices for the live plans in USD, GBP and EUR, monthly and yearly. Put their IDs in `PLANS`.
4. **TD admin → Finance → Membership Plans:** read the plan IDs into `TD_PLAN_IDS`. Confirm the TD API's member get and update field names (`/api/v2/user/get`, `/api/v2/user/update`, `subscription_id`).
5. **Deploy and connect the Worker:**
   - Create the `td-billing` KV store and deploy the Worker.
   - Add the Stripe webhook (events: checkout session completed, checkout session async payment succeeded, customer subscription updated, customer subscription deleted).
   - The owner copies the webhook signing secret into the Secrets Store as `STRIPE_BILLING_WEBHOOK_SECRET`.
6. **Test with the owner's test members:**
   - Featured test member upgrades by card at a temporary £1 price, then refund.
   - A wallet payment from a phone.
   - A cancellation in the portal moves the member back to Basic.
7. **Switch the pricing page and dashboard buttons to the new widget.** The platform's own card form stays in place for existing subscriptions until they move.

**Undo:** put the old buttons back; Stripe subscriptions keep running and can be cancelled from Stripe.
